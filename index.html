    Our story

    Born on the 99, raised in Eugene.

    HWY 99 started with a pickup truck, a notebook of farm visits, and a stubborn belief that a dispensary should feel like a good roadside stop — honest, unhurried, a little weird, and worth pulling over for.

    Today we partner with a small circle of Willamette Valley growers up and down the corridor. We know who pulled the trim shears, what soil the plant came up in, and how the cure went. That's the only way it gets on the shelf.
