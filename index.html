Our story

Born off Exit 99.

HWY 99 started with a pickup truck, a notebook of farm visits, and a stubborn belief that a dispensary should feel like a good roadside stop — honest, unhurried, a little weird, and worth pulling over for.

Today we partner with a small circle of growers up and down the corridor. We know who pulled the trim shears, what soil the plant came up in, and how the cure went. That's the only way we put it on the shelf.
